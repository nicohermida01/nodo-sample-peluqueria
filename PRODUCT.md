# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Two audiences, in this order:

1. **Salon visitors** (the demo's simulated end users): people in Buenos Aires looking for a haircut, color, treatment, event styling or barbering. Almost all arrive on a phone from Instagram or Google Maps. Their job: feel in seconds whether the salon's style is "for me", see real results, pick a service, see what it costs and how long it takes, and book in two or three taps.
2. **Template buyers** (the real commercial audience): owners of independent Argentine hair salons and barbershops, 1 to 6 chairs, who today get clients through Instagram and WhatsApp. They see the demo on their phone from a link Estudio Nodo sends them. Their job: judge whether this is a finished site where they only swap photos, prices and team.

## Product Purpose

Demo site and resellable template by Estudio Nodo for a **fictitious** salon, "NUDO Estudio de Pelo" (Palermo, CABA). Two goals: show the salon's work and style, and convert visits into bookings through an external booking system or WhatsApp with a prefilled message. Success for the visitor: reaching "Reservar" with service, price and duration known. Success for the buyer: perceiving a finished, rebrandable product.

## Positioning

An *auteur* salon, not a chain: long appointments, a diagnosis before every service, few clients per day, clear "desde" prices and visible conditions. The site has to make that difference tangible (time and judgment), not just state it.

## Operating Context

- Traffic: mobile first, from Instagram bio links and Google Maps listings.
- Booking happens outside the site: an external system (Fresha, Booksy, AgendaPro; empty in the demo) or WhatsApp `wa.me` with a prefilled message ("Hola! Quiero turno para [servicio]").
- Rebranding workflow: brand data and global copy in `src/config/site.ts`; services, team and gallery in Astro content collections (`src/content/`).

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Static Astro 7 site with Tailwind 4; zero JS outside small islands (filters, lightbox, before/after slider).
- Pages planned: Home, Servicios, Galería, Equipo, legal pages (privacy, booking and cancellation policy). None built yet.
- Commercial rules (sample values): Tuesday/Wednesday 15% off color with listed conditions; informational gift cards only (request via WhatsApp); 30% deposit for services over 2 h; free cancellation up to 24 h before; 10% off paying cash or bank transfer.
- No in-site booking or payment, no card data, no functional gift card sales.
- Targets: WCAG AA, Lighthouse ≥ 95 in all four categories, local SEO ("peluquería en Palermo", "balayage en Palermo"), JSON-LD HairSalon + Service/Offer.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: "NUDO Estudio de Pelo". Tagline: "Cortes y color pensados para vos, sin apuro."
- Voice: close, relaxed, with flair but professional, in Rioplatense Spanish (voseo).
- Visible footer notice while the demo is live: "Sitio de demostración: la peluquería, el equipo y los precios son ficticios." (`site.demoNotice.enabled`).
- Visual references in `referencias/` are binding for feel (see CLAUDE.md); never copy their text, logos, photos or brands.

## Evidence on Hand

- All salon data, team (4 stylists), services (19), prices and gallery (24 works, 4 before/after) are fictitious samples in `src/config/site.ts` and `src/content/`.
- All photos are labeled placeholders generated by `scripts/placeholder.mjs`; no real client photos exist.
- Testimonials in `site.ts` are sample copy flagged `placeholder: true` and must be labeled as such on the page.
- No real reviews, ratings, client counts, press or awards exist: never invent them.

## Product Principles

1. Booking is two or three taps away from anywhere: service → price and duration → book.
2. Show the work before talking about it: photos of results carry the persuasion.
3. Honesty is part of the brand: "desde" prices, visible promo conditions, placeholders labeled, no misleading before/after.
4. Everything rebrandable lives in data, never in components.
5. Calm over noise: the site behaves like the salon (unhurried), with no popups, autoplay carousels or tricks.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA: contrast, visible focus, descriptive alt text from the collections, keyboard-operable lightbox, tabs and before/after slider, touch targets ≥ 44px, full `prefers-reduced-motion` support. Photography should show diverse hair types and lengths; copy avoids gendering the visitor where possible (the salon serves women, men and kids).
